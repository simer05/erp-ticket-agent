# Procedure FIN-04: Bank reconciliation differences

Applies to unmatched items between the bank statement and the ledger.

## Small FX rounding differences
If the absolute difference between bank amount and ledger amount is SGD 5.00 or less and the cause is FX rounding,
post an adjustment for exactly the difference to the FX rounding account. The item is then matched.

## Everything else
If the difference is larger than SGD 5.00, or the cause is unknown, do not post an adjustment. Escalate to the finance team with the amounts.
