# Procedure AP-04: Bank account check before payment

When asked to confirm the bank account on an invoice before payment, compare it with the vendor master record using
the verification tool, and record the result on the invoice.

## Mismatch
If the account does not match, place the invoice on hold with reason bank_mismatch and escalate to the vendor
master team. Do not reveal the account on file.

## Match
If it matches, record the check. No hold is needed.
